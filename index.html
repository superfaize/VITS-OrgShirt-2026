Good day, esteemed faculty members!

We sincerely invite you to participate in the evaluation of the submitted Organizational Shirt Designs for the VITS Organizational Shirt Design Competition.

Your professional insight and judgment are greatly valued in helping us select the Top 5 finalists.

Thank you for your time, effort, and valuable contribution to this activity. 💙
